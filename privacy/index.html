Welcome to Cozy Paws! 🐾

Cozy Paws is developed and operated by Appocalyse. This Privacy Policy explains how Cozy Paws handles information when you play our mobile game.

Cozy Paws is intended for players of all ages. We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the information collected through the game.

1. Information We Collect

Cozy Paws does not require players to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The game stores gameplay information, such as virtual coins, inventory, game settings, and progress, locally on your device.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly from players through ordinary gameplay.

2. How We Use Information

Locally stored gameplay information is used to save your progress, maintain your virtual coin balance, remember your inventory, and provide the game's features.

We do not use the game to run advertising campaigns or collect analytics through separate analytics services.

3. In-App Purchases

Cozy Paws offers optional purchases of virtual coins using real money through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play and are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

We do not directly collect or store complete payment card details through the game. Google may process transaction and payment information to complete purchases, prevent fraud, and provide purchase-related services.

Virtual coins are digital items used within Cozy Paws and have no independent real-world monetary value.

For questions about a purchase or a payment issue, contact us at appocalyse44445555@mail.com .

Refund requests may need to be submitted through Google Play, subject to applicable law and Google's policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Cozy Paws is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13.

The game does not require children to provide personal information to play. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through ordinary gameplay.

If our information practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy and implement any notices, consent procedures, or other safeguards required by applicable law.

Parents and guardians who have questions about children's privacy may contact us using the email address provided below.

5. Third-Party Services

Cozy Paws uses Google Play to distribute the game and process in-app purchases.

Google may independently collect and process information in accordance with its own policies when you download the game or make a purchase.

We do not intentionally integrate separate advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services into the game.

Gameplay progress and related game data are stored locally on your device.

This information may be lost if you uninstall the game, clear its application data, reset your device, or experience device failure.

You can generally remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game, subject to your device's behavior.

Google retains purchase and transaction information according to its own policies and applicable legal requirements.
